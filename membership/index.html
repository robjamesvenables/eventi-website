Four steps. Nothing to download.

Questions

Good to know.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. They point their phone camera at the keyring and their page opens. That's it.

What if a member loses their keyring?

Give them a new one and your team links it to their profile. Their visits, perks and rewards stay exactly as they were.

We have more than one venue. Does it work?

Yes - that's where it shines. One keyring works at all your venues, each venue gets its own page, and your team sees a member's visits everywhere.

Can other venues see my members?

Never. Each venue only sees its own members, visits and details. If a guest is a Key holder somewhere else, you'll never know where.

What if a guest already has a Mavis Key?

Your team scans it and adds your venue in one tap. When they scan it, your venue is on their phone with your own perks. If they'd like your keyring too - or one for their partner - give them one.

Can it match our brand?

Yes. Your logo on the keyrings and the page, and your own colours if you want them - or keep the clean Mavis look.

Is it legal to email our members?

Your team must ask every new member "Happy for us to send you offers?" - sign-up can't finish without a yes or no. Members can change it on their own page at any time, and your email list only includes the yeses.

Do we need the full Mavis app?

No. Mavis Key works on its own. If you want more later, we switch it on - nothing to move, nothing to set up again.

How long does it take to set up?

About a day to set up your venues, perks and page. The keyrings take a little longer while they're engraved - you can start with the page straight away.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many venues and keyrings you need. Message us and we'll send you a simple price - no long contract talk.
