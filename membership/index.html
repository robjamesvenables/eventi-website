Questions

Good to know.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No app store. They scan the keyring and your app opens. One tap saves it to their home screen, with your name under the icon, just like any other app.

We already have our own app. Will it clash?

No. Keep your app for bookings. This is your members club: on their phone it shows your logo with the name "Membership", so guests never mix them up. You can also add a "My membership" button to your app that opens it.

How does the member discount work with Megatix?

Make one member code in Megatix (or any ticket site), type the same code in Mavis once, and members see it on every event with a Copy button. Mavis fills it in for you next time.

Can my marketing team change the page themselves?

Yes. Photos, events, deals, colours and buttons, all from a laptop or phone, with a live preview. Guests see it the moment you save.

How do we send emails to members?

From your own email, for free. Mavis lists only the members who said yes to offers and splits them into groups your email allows. Copy, paste, send.

How do we get more keyrings?

Mavis counts them for you. When you get down to 15, managers see a reminder. Tap "Order more keyrings", pick a bundle, and the order comes straight to us. They're made with your logo and arrive in 1 to 2 weeks.

What if we run out of keyrings?

Guests can still join. Your team signs them up as normal and sends their app on WhatsApp. They get their keyring on their next visit.

What if a member loses their keyring?

Give them a new one and your team links it to their profile. Their visits, perks and rewards stay exactly as they were.

We have several restaurants and bars. Does it work?

Yes. One keyring works everywhere, every place gets its own page, and your team sees a member's visits across all of them.

Can it match our brand?

Yes. Your logo on the keyrings and the page, and your own colours, picked in a few taps.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many places and keyrings you need. Message us and we'll send you a simple price.
