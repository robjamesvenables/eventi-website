Talk to Robbie

Good to know.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the key with their phone camera and your page opens. That's it.

Can other venues see my members?

Never. Each venue only sees its own members, visits and details. If a guest is a Key holder at another venue, you'll never know which one.

What if a guest already has a key from somewhere else?

Your team scans it and adds your venue in one tap. The guest keeps one key, and their phone shows your page with your perks. If they'd like your own keyring as well, give them one.

What if a guest loses their key?

Give them a new one. Their memberships move across and the old key stops working.

What do the keys look like?

Solid wood with your logo, a QR code and the Mavis Key mark engraved, on a leather strap.

Is this part of Mavis?

Yes. Mavis Key runs on Mavis, the team app for hospitality. Start with Mavis Key, and add the rest of Mavis whenever you're ready.
