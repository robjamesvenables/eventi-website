THE FOUNDER

Rob Venables has spent more than 20 years turning complex operations into simple, working technology - in banking with Bank of America and in telecoms, building mobile apps for global brands, and for the last 12 years in Bali, inside its hospitality scene. Along the way he led products and marketing at two Amazon partner agencies, growing brands on Amazon with US clients every day - learning how the world's biggest retailer uses data, reviews and rewards to turn one-time buyers into loyal, returning customers. The same thinking is behind Mavis.

At Potato Head and Luna Beach Club he saw the same three gaps in every venue. Guests spent money and left as strangers, with nothing to bring them back. Teams ran on scattered WhatsApp groups, paper and spreadsheets. And bookings relied on costly outside systems that didn't fit how venues really sell loungers, cabanas, tables and treatments. Mavis began at Luna - one beach club and restaurant as the testing ground - with one answer for each gap: Members, Team and Bookings. A hotel is really many venues under one roof, so the progression is natural. Built in Bali, for hospitality, by someone who has run the technology behind it.

THE MAVIS PLATFORM

1Mavis Members

Membership and marketing in one app - plus a guest guide for everyone

Members app earned · premium · regulars and VIPs

  • Given with a Trans-branded keyring (or digital card) by staff
  • Gold member card, visit card and reward progress
  • Perks and member-only deals at every outlet
  • Recognised at every Trans hotel; VIP set by the hotel

Guest guide open to everyone · every stay and visit

  • Scan any QR in rooms or on menus - nothing to fill in
  • What's on, pool, gym, spa and kids club times
  • Menus, floor guide, one-tap WhatsApp to reception
  • Natural next step: "How do I become a member?"
Guest guide → Member (keyring) → VIP  ·  every guest informed, the best guests rewarded

2Mavis Team

One app for the whole team
  • Team chat and feed - replaces scattered WhatsApp groups
  • Maintenance requests: report, sort, fix, with photos
  • SOPs, schedules and task boards in one place
  • Simple enough for any new starter on day one

3Mavis Bookings

Booking tools made for venues
  • Tap-a-spot: guests pick the exact lounger, cabana or table on a map - mocked for 28th Sky, an alternative to Chope
  • Spa rooms: treatments and therapists across rooms on one screen - running as a demo for Sundari Wellness
  • Padel courts: a visual court calendar, book in a tap

10 REASONS FOR THE PARTNERSHIP

Surabaya as the pilot. A busy F&B hotel is the ideal proving ground: turn walk-in diners into known, returning members.
Prove it, then roll it out. One hotel first with real numbers, then the same set-up at every Trans hotel.
One keyring, every hotel. A member made in Surabaya is recognised in Bali and across the group - loyalty to Trans, not one outlet.
Membership that markets you. Every scan opens a Trans-branded page with today's deals, events and menus - marketing in the guest's pocket.
Reasons to come back. A visit card with a reward, plus perks that work at the bill: F&B and spa discounts, kids club, late check-out.
Easy for staff. One front-desk screen: Paying, Check card, New member, Search. Sign-up in under a minute, app sent on WhatsApp.
Data Trans owns. Who visits, how often, which outlet, new versus returning - reports per hotel or for the whole group.
Trans stays in control. Marketing updates photos, deals, events and menus from the back office - no developer, no waiting.
A guest guide for every guest. One QR on rooms, menus and table talkers replaces paper and slow apps, and leads guests to membership.
A direct line to the founder. Trans joins a small group of hand-picked launch partners, working with Rob directly - with first access to new features and a real say in what's built next.
