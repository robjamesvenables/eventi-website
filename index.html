✦ Features
The operating system for hospitality.
Everything your venue needs—from planning events to managing kitchens, SOPs, vendors and daily operations—all in one connected platform.
Event Plans clients actually read
Professional, real-time Event Plans shared with one link—no login required. Updates instantly, so no one's ever working from an old version.
Every artist rider, organised
Technical specs, stage plots, dressing rooms and guest lists—one card per artist, sorted before they arrive.
Every supplier in one place
Quotes, invoices, contacts and approvals organised from first enquiry to payment—no more digging through email chains.
Your whole schedule, one glance
Month, list and day views, auto-populated from every Event Plan—so your calendar is never out of sync with reality.
One notepad, the whole team reads
A single shared entry per day, autosaved as you type—copy straight to WhatsApp in one tap.
Share without screenshots
Send any Event Plan, rundown or note straight to WhatsApp, fully formatted and ready to read.
Know your profit before the event begins
Track costs, revenue and margins automatically as your event develops.
Every client, every event
Build stronger relationships with a complete history of enquiries, events and communications.
Keep every department aligned
Drag-and-drop tasks across events, training, maintenance and daily operations—all from one shared board.
One source of truth for your menus
Recipes, allergens and live menu updates stay synced between kitchen and front of house.
Your entire team's handbook
Store procedures, training and documents so everyone always has the latest version.
